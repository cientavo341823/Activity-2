# From Information to Visual Communication
  This two-page infographic presents the importance of Civil Engineering and its contribution to society. The first page highlights how civil engineers help build and maintain essential infrastructure such as roads, bridges, water and sanitation systems, disaster-resilient structures, and sustainable developments, emphasizing their role in creating safer, healthier, and more connected communities. The second page presents a graphical comparison of students taking Civil Engineering alongside Computer Science, Electrical Engineering, and Architecture, using percentages to show the distribution of students among the four courses. Overall, the infographic demonstrates that Civil Engineering is not only a technical profession focused on construction and design but also an important discipline that contributes to economic development, public safety, environmental sustainability, and the quality of everyday life.
